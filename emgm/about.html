About us

Our story

e.MGm Food Supplement Trading was registered with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) on November 27, 2001, with its business address at 14 Diamond Street corner Sapphire Street, Southville Subdivision, Barangay Holy Spirit, Quezon City. With nearly 20 years of experience in the health industry, our owner is committed to providing quality health and wellness products through a direct-selling business model. Our products include health coffee, food supplements, and health juices manufactured by registered laboratories. Today, e.MGm Food Supplement Trading has approximately 36 outlets nationwide, continuing its mission to make health and wellness products accessible to more Filipinos.

Mission

To make everyday coffee a little more interesting with herbs and superfoods people already know.

What makes us different

Each blend pairs real coffee with plant ingredients such as barley, moringa, maca root and mangosteen.
